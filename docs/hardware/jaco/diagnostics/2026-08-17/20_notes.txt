Kinova JACO - Passive USB diagnostic test

Test condition:
- No robot movement was commanded.
- 24 V robot power disconnected.
- Robot power switch OFF.
- Joystick was not used.
- Only the USB-B connection between the JACO and Ubuntu laptop was tested.

Initial result:
- No new device appeared in lsusb after connecting the JACO while unpowered.
- The lsusb before/after comparison produced no differences.

Safety constraint:
- The robot must not move during these identification tests.
- No Kinova driver, ROS control node, MoveIt configuration, motion example, joint command, Cartesian command, or joystick command was executed.
